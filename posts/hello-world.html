Hello, world! Why I started this blog

Sep 30, 2025 • 3 min read

Welcome! I finally decided to start writing publicly. Expect short, useful posts about software engineering, design systems, and mindful productivity. I’ll share things I learn, problems I run into, and solutions that worked for me.

Here’s roughly what I plan to cover:

  • Guides and checklists I wish I had when starting something new
  • Deep dives into tools and techniques that improved my workflow
  • Notes and snippets I frequently copy-paste into projects

This site is intentionally simple: plain HTML, CSS, and a sprinkle of JavaScript. It’s fast, accessible, and deploys anywhere. I might move to a generator later, but for now, simplicity wins.

If you want me to write about something specific, reach out — I read everything.
